Platform Notes

Indexing Pipeline
The indexing pipeline creates page records, section records, and hybrid lexical scoring artifacts without any vector database.
Title matches and keyword overlap are fused with BM25 and TF-IDF scores to rank evidence.

Enterprise Access
Single sign-on support is available on the enterprise plan only.
Audit export automation can be enabled for enterprise workspaces during onboarding.

Analytics Exports
The analytics module exports CSV reports every morning at 06:00 UTC.
Disaster recovery snapshots are retained for 30 days in the primary environment.
